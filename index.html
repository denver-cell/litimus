    Under the hood

    What Litimus actually measures.

    No black box score. Every signal below is visible in your report.

    01 Burstiness

    Human sentences swing between short and long; AI output tends toward a narrow, even rhythm. Low variance raises the score.

    02 Stock transitions

    Phrases like "moreover," "it is important to note," and "in conclusion" cluster far more densely in generated text.

    03 Vocabulary spread

    A low ratio of unique words to total words suggests safe, repeated word choices rather than a wide natural vocabulary.

    04 Phrase repetition

    Repeated three-word sequences across a passage are rare in human drafts and common in generated ones.

    05 Punctuation fingerprint

    Overuse of em dashes and semicolons, or an absence of the small errors humans make, both shift the score.

    06 Structural symmetry

    Paragraphs of near-identical length and matching openers read as templated rather than composed.

    Fine print, upfront

    Detectors are evidence, not verdicts.

    We'd rather lose a sale than oversell what statistical detection can do. Every report on Litimus carries the same caveats we're stating here.

    • 01Heavily edited AI text and unusual human styles can both land in the middle of the range — treat borderline scores as inconclusive.
    • 02No score from Litimus, or any competitor, should be the sole basis for an academic or employment penalty.
    • 03Non-native English writing is sometimes flagged more often; we tune against this, but it isn't solved industry-wide.
    • 04Short passages under 25 words don't carry enough signal for a stable read, and we say so instead of guessing.

    Questions

    Before you paste anything in.

    Can Litimus tell me for certain that something was written by AI?
    No — and no detector on the market can, honestly. Litimus gives you a probability based on writing patterns, plus the exact reasons behind the number, so you can weigh it yourself rather than take a black-box verdict.
    How is the student plan verified?
    Through a third-party student-verification service (the kind Spotify and Amazon use for student pricing) rather than a bare .edu check — it recognizes academic domains and enrollment records across most countries, not just the US, and falls back to a student-ID upload if your institution doesn't use a standard email pattern. We re-verify once a year.
    Do you store the text I scan?
    Free and Student scans are processed in-session and discarded. Pro and Team plans can opt into saved report history for their own records — off by default.
    Does heavy editing of AI output fool the detector?
    Often, yes, to a degree — this is a known limit of statistical detection industry-wide, which is why we show a confidence range and margin notes instead of a bare pass/fail stamp.
    What if I just need more words for one deadline?
    Buy a one-time day pass ($3) instead of upgrading your plan — it adds 10,000 words to your limit for 24 hours, works on top of Free or any paid tier, and doesn't auto-renew or commit you to anything ongoing.
    Is there an API?
    Yes, on the Team & API plan — REST endpoints for scanning, batch jobs, and webhook callbacks, billed on included volume plus metered overage.
