1. **Your syllabus states the policy.** If AI assistance was never addressed in writing, a score isn’t grounds for anything.
2. **You have a baseline.** In-class writing or earlier submissions to compare against — not just the one essay in isolation.
3. **You are scanning full submissions, not excerpts.** Short passages are too unstable for a fair read.
4. **You have blocked time to read the reasons, not just the numbers.** A stack of scores with no context is how honest students get treated unfairly.
5. **You know your process for a borderline result.** Decide before you start, not while looking at one particular student’s name.
6. **You are ready to talk to students before concluding anything**, per <a href="/for/teachers">the five-step process</a>.
