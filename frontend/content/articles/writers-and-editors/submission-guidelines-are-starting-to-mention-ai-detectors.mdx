Two patterns are showing up in submission guidelines: a disclosure requirement (“state whether any part of this was AI-assisted”), or a house scan before acceptance. Neither is universal yet, but both are becoming more common.

Practical steps either way:

- **Keep your own draft history.** The same version-history trail that protects a student protects a submitting writer — see <a href="/for/students/keeping-proof-your-essay-is-yours">keeping proof your essay is yours</a> for the mechanics, which apply just as well here.
- **Run your own check before submitting**, especially for pieces in a tight, formulaic form (press releases, structured reviews) that can read as templated to a statistical detector with no AI involved at all.
- **Read the guideline’s actual wording.** “No AI-generated content” and “disclose AI assistance” are different rules — know which one you’re agreeing to before you hit submit.
