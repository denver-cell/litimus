## House style already looks like what a detector flags

“It is important to note,” “furthermore,” consistent passive voice, evenly structured paragraphs — long-standing conventions of academic writing, adopted for clarity and consistency across a field, not because of any AI tool. A detector reading this prose is reading decades of style-guide convention.

## The methods section is usually the most uniform part

Methods and procedure sections in particular tend to follow a fixed, near-template structure across an entire field, because that consistency is what makes a paper’s methods comparable to others’. Expect this section specifically to score higher than a discussion or introduction, regardless of authorship.

## What to check if a journal or supervisor raises a score

Whether the specific claims, citations and results are accurate and specific to the actual research — a detector cannot assess any of that, and it is the part that actually matters for academic integrity in this context.

## If your field’s own conventions are being read as suspicious

That is worth raising directly with an editor or supervisor as a genre issue, not something to solve by deliberately writing less like a published paper in your field.
